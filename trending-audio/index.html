Week of Oct 30, 2026

Trending audio this week

12 Reels and TikTok sounds that are trending right now, each paired with a format a restaurant or local business can film. Every item links the public chart or report it came from.

Licensing note. Business and brand accounts often get a restricted music library. On TikTok, that's the Commercial Music Library. On Instagram, some songs aren't available to business profiles. A sound that trends on a personal account may be missing, muted, or removed on yours. Check that the sound is available on your account before you post. When in doubt, use the original Suno prompt on each card (check Suno's current terms for commercial use).

Instagram Reels

What You Waiting For?

Gwen Stefani

Built for quick-cut story Reels: lyrics appear word by word over 'take that chance' moments. A natural fit for an owner or team origin story.

Format idea: Format 16 (owner origin story): first day photo, empty room, opening night, full room today. One cut per lyric line.

License-safe alternative: Suno prompt idea

upbeat pop-rock build, hopeful, driving drums, instrumental, 20 seconds, clear beat drops every 2 seconds for quick cuts

Source: Buffer, 13 trending sounds on Instagram, Oct 2026

Instagram Reels

Upside Down

Diana Ross

Used for disco-flavored before-and-after transitions. Swap the outfit reveal for a plate, a fade, or a fully detailed car.

Format idea: Format 8 (before & after): raw ingredients, camera flip on the beat, finished plate.

License-safe alternative: Suno prompt idea

70s disco funk groove, bright strings, four-on-the-floor, instrumental, 15 seconds, big transition hit at 7 seconds

Source: Buffer, 13 trending sounds on Instagram, Oct 2026

Instagram Reels

Windows in Brazil (Sped Up)

VHM4D, ahh

The '0% to 100% loading screen' trend. Shows something being built step by step, which maps onto any build-a-dish or service Reel.

Format idea: Format 5 (how we make it): 0% empty plate, 25% base, 50% sauce, 75% toppings, 100% hero shot. Add the % as on-screen text.

License-safe alternative: Suno prompt idea

retro computer startup chime into sped-up playful electronic beat, instrumental, 15 seconds, rising energy

Source: CreatorDB weekly chart, Oct 28, 2026 (also in Buffer, Oct 2026)

Instagram Reels

Autumn Journey

Andrea Vanzo

A calm instrumental used for slow, handmade autumn content. It suits fall menus, bakeries, and cozy cafe Reels.

Format idea: Format 12 (ASMR dish close-up) or Format 5: slow pours, dough folding, steam, a seasonal latte. Keep the cuts slow.

License-safe alternative: Suno prompt idea

gentle autumn solo piano with soft strings, warm, slow, instrumental, 30 seconds, no vocals

Source: Buffer, 13 trending sounds on Instagram, Oct 2026 (also CreatorDB, Oct 28)

Instagram Reels

Last Train to London

Electric Light Orchestra

Soundtracks 'a week of looks' quick-cut montages; CreatorDB tracks a version under the 'the drink makes the fit' trend. Good for a weekly menu or cocktail list.

Format idea: Format 13 (top three to order): Monday to Friday, one drink or special per day, one cut per beat.

License-safe alternative: Suno prompt idea

late 70s disco pop groove, string stabs, bouncy bass, instrumental, 20 seconds, steady beat for day-by-day cuts

Source: Buffer, Oct 2026; CreatorDB, Oct 28, 2026

cozy acoustic folk, fingerpicked guitar, warm and homey, instrumental, 20 seconds

Source: CreatorDB weekly chart, Oct 28, 2026
