Questions

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Every format, template idea, and prompt idea on this site is free to use. The site is supported by a free weekly newsletter and, clearly labeled sponsor slots. Learn about sponsoring on the sponsor page.

Will these Reels guarantee more customers?

No. These are proven formats in the sense that public marketing guides recommend them, and each card links its source. Results depend on your business, your footage, and many other factors.

Do I need fancy gear?

No gear is required by any format here. Every template is written to be filmable on a phone and edited in a free app like CapCut.

Can I use Suno music in my Reels?

Check Suno's current terms and your plan before publishing commercially. We offer prompt ideas only; licensing is between you and Suno.

Why are Instagram examples links instead of embeds?

Example posts belong to their creators. We link to them with credit rather than embedding them, and we never re-host anyone's content.

Where do the formats come from?

From public guides by Sycamore, Hootsuite, Later, ACH Media, Hero Content, and Superdirector. Each format page lists the exact sources and section numbers.
