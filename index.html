The formats

These are ideas drawn from public marketing guides, which are linked on each card. Results vary, and no format guarantees views or sales.

1. Behind-the-scenes prep

Best for: Restaurants, bakeries

CapCut / Suno angle: CapCut beat-synced cut; Suno upbeat 15s bed

Real-world example: a public Instagram post (Wandering Vine, KC restaurant). View post on Instagram (credit: the account owner).
