Restaurant Reel Ideas

Stuck on what to post? These restaurant Reel ideas come from public marketing guides (linked on every format page). Pick one, film the shot list on your phone, and edit in CapCut.

How to pick a restaurant Reel idea

  • New in town or want reach? Try a “Get discovered” format such as behind-the-scenes prep or empty room vs full service.
  • Want orders tonight? Try tonight's special or dish close-up hero.
  • Want regulars to feel at home? Try meet the team or the owner origin story.
  • Hiring? Show a real shift.

A simple weekly rhythm (suggestion)

One discovery Reel, one trust Reel, one orders Reel. Batch-film on a slow afternoon. This is a starting point, not a rule; adjust to what your guests respond to.

01Get discoveredReal example

Behind-the-scenes prep

“6:45 a.m. Nobody sees this part.”

Film morning prep / setup before opening.

Best for: Restaurants, bakeries

Shot list & script

  2. Hands on prep list
  3. Knife work macro
  4. Ovens / burners firing
  5. Wide of set room, open sign

Hook: “6:45 a.m. Nobody sees this part.” · [Text] What happens before we open → [VO] Every day starts here… → [Text] Doors open at 11. Come hungry.
