Salon Instagram Reel Ideas

Salon Reel ideas you can film between clients. Every format links the public guide that recommends it; the salon twists below are our suggestions.

Salon twists on each format

  • Before & after: same chair, same light, same angle for both shots (get the client's OK first).
  • Myth-busting: “Myth: you have to wash your hair every day.” Answer from your stylist's experience.
  • Questions we get every week: pricing ranges, how long appointments take, how to prep.
  • Meet the team: each stylist's specialty in one line.

Always get written consent before filming a client, and respect anyone who says no.
