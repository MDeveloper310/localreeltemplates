Market haul / supplier Reel template

Delivery or market run; where ingredients come from. Best for: Farm-to-table restaurants.

Real-world example: a public Instagram post (Normandie Napa, 'No food vendor'). View post on Instagram (credit: the account owner).

Hook (first 1–2 seconds)

“Where your [dish] actually comes from.”

Shot list

  1. Farm / market / delivery
  2. Hands choosing produce
  3. Back in kitchen
  4. On the plate

Script skeleton

[Map sticker: supplier] → [VO] who they are → [Text] Local, on purpose

CapCut steps

  1. Import clips in shot order; set canvas to 9:16.
  2. Apply the edit idea: map sticker.
  3. Add on-screen text for the hook in the first second; run Auto captions.
  4. Add your music (Suno track or in-app audio); trim to the beat.
  5. Export 1080p; add a cover frame with the hook text.

Suno prompt idea

warm folk, fingerpicked guitar, instrumental, 15–30 seconds, clean intro, no vocals, loopable ending

Check Suno's current terms for commercial use.
