Owner origin story Reel template

Owner tells why they opened. Best for: Family restaurants, local shops.

Hook (first 1–2 seconds)

“Why I opened this place.”

Shot list

  1. Old photo / first day
  2. Owner to camera
  3. Hands at work
  4. Full room today

Script skeleton

[VO] The moment I decided… → what it means now → [Text] Thank you, [town]

CapCut steps

  1. Import clips in shot order; set canvas to 9:16.
  2. Apply the edit idea: Suno acoustic bed + voiceover.
  3. Add on-screen text for the hook in the first second; run Auto captions.
  4. Add your music (Suno track or in-app audio); trim to the beat.
  5. Export 1080p; add a cover frame with the hook text.

Suno prompt idea

gentle acoustic guitar, heartfelt, instrumental, 15–30 seconds, clean intro, no vocals, loopable ending
