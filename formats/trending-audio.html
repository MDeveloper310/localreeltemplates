Trending audio Reel template

Put business footage to currently trending audio. Best for: Any.

Need a sound? See trending audio this week, with a format idea and a license-safe Suno prompt for each.

Hook (first 1–2 seconds)

“[Match the trend's hook]”

Shot list

  1. Pick audio from the Reels audio browser
  2. Map your footage to the beats
  3. Keep the trend's format, swap in your business

Script skeleton

Follow the trend's structure; end with your name + location

CapCut steps

  1. Import clips in shot order; set canvas to 9:16.
  2. Apply the edit idea: Pick from template library.
  3. Add on-screen text for the hook in the first second; run Auto captions.
  4. Add your music (Suno track or in-app audio); trim to the beat.
  5. Export 1080p; add a cover frame with the hook text.

Suno prompt idea
