Format 03 · Build trust

Meet the team Reel template

Same 3 questions per staffer; end with 'say hi'. Best for: Restaurants, bars, hotels.

Real-world example: a public Instagram post (Lititz Springs Inn, pub & inn). View post on Instagram (credit: the account owner).

Hook (first 1–2 seconds)

“Meet the people who make your [dish].”

Shot list

  1. Staffer framed chest-up
  2. Q1: Favorite thing on the menu?
  3. Q2: Best part of the job?
  4. Q3: Your go-to order?
  5. Wave + 'say hi'

Script skeleton

[Name card] → 3 answers → [Text] Come say hi to ___ this week

CapCut steps

  1. Import clips in shot order; set canvas to 9:16.
  2. Apply the edit idea: name-card intro.
  3. Add on-screen text for the hook in the first second; run Auto captions.
  4. Add your music (Suno track or in-app audio); trim to the beat.
  5. Export 1080p; add a cover frame with the hook text.

Suno prompt idea

bright indie pop, friendly, instrumental, 15–30 seconds, clean intro, no vocals, loopable ending
